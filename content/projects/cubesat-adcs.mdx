## Overview

A full CubeSat attitude control stack: sensors, actuators, simulation, and
control algorithms. This extends the attitude control and simulation work
from [Spacecraft Dynamics Simulation](/projects/spacecraft-dynamics-simulation)
and [Balancing Triangle](/projects/balancing-triangle) into a full CubeSat-class
ADCS, including sensor fusion from IMU, sun sensor, and magnetometer data,
reaction-wheel and magnetorquer actuation, and a control stack validated in
simulation before moving to hardware.
