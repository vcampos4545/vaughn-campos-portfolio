## Overview

Development of a CubeSat attitude control stack including sensors, actuators,
simulation, and control algorithms. This project extends the attitude control
and simulation work from [Spacecraft Dynamics Simulation](/projects/spacecraft-dynamics-simulation)
and [Balancing Triangle](/projects/balancing-triangle) into a full CubeSat-class
ADCS: sensor fusion from IMU/sun-sensor/magnetometer data, reaction-wheel and
magnetorquer actuation, and a control stack validated in simulation before
moving to hardware.
